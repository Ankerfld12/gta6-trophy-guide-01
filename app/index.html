Roadmap to the Platinum

Six phases, ordered from the official descriptions: story first (it unlocks everything else and holds the flagged one-chance moments), strands and partner work while the map is fresh, then the Leonida-wide sets, the skill targets, the money game, and the 100% counter that closes the list. Hour figures are rough planning estimates inferred from the counts in the official descriptions (250 stunts, 100 locations, 42 range challenges) — they are estimates, not promises or measured times.
